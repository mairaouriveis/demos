How to use this brand kit

Purpose

Ready-made images and copy to build a Fundraise Up campaign for Human Concern International fast. Every image here is also in the assets/ folder, ready to upload.

What's inside

  • Logo (200×200, transparent)
  • Three campaign images, each with title + message copy
  • A matching thank-you email for each campaign
  • Brand colours
  • Organization & charity registration info

Sizing

Campaign images (1248×702) fit a campaign modal or a full campaign page. The logo suits the donor portal and receipts. Everything is pre-sized — no cropping needed.

Steps

  1. Log into your demo account's admin dashboard.
  2. Find your campaign, or create one.
  3. Pick one option below (image + title + message) and use it for the campaign modal/page and its thank-you email — same image throughout.
  4. Apply the brand colours to the Donate Button element and email customization (and any other Elements).
  5. Use the logo and org info for the donor portal and receipts.

Reminder

Demos stay in test mode — no real charges are ever processed.

Campaign options

Each option is one complete package: image, message, and thank-you email. For every email, swap the subject line to "Thank you for your recurring support!" for recurring gifts — the rest of the email stays the same.

Hope and Healing International

Fundraise Up demo brand kit — org code ABPNEHGY

Purpose

Ready-made images and copy so you can build a Fundraise Up campaign fast. Every image here is also saved in assets/ for upload to the dashboard.

What's inside

The logo, three campaign images (each with matching message copy), a thank-you email for each campaign, brand colors, organization info, and spare images.

Sizing

Campaign images fit a campaign modal or a full campaign page. The logo and spare images suit the donor portal. Everything is pre-sized — no cropping needed.

Steps

  1. Log into your demo account's admin dashboard.
  2. Find your campaign, or create one.
  3. Pick one CTA option (image + title + message) and use it for both the campaign modal/page and its matching thank-you email — keep the same image throughout.
  4. Apply the brand colors to the Donate Button element and email customization (and any other elements).
  5. Use the logo, spare images, and org info for the donor portal and receipts.
This demo stays in test mode — no real transactions are ever processed.
